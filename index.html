Discover Bakuchiol

Plant-based anti-age active

Bakuchiol.
Gentle, plant-based renewal.

A plant-based alternative to retinol, at 1% in our Advanced Peptide Anti-Age Cream.

3 key benefits of Bakuchiol

  • Anti-aging without irritation: reduces wrinkles and fine lines by boosting collagen, without causing redness or peeling.
  • Radiance and fewer dark spots: fades dark spots and discoloration, for a more even skin tone.
  • Anti-acne action: its anti-inflammatory properties calm the skin and reduce breakouts.

Who uses it?

  • People with sensitive skin: those with rosacea, eczema or atopic dermatitis who can't tolerate classic retinol.
  • Pregnant and breastfeeding women: it is the completely safe alternative, as retinoids are strictly avoided during these periods.
  • Beginners in anti-aging: those who want to start a powerful routine without retinol's “adjustment period” (retinization).
  • Anyone who wants morning care: because it doesn't break down in sunlight, it can be used for anti-aging in the morning too.
